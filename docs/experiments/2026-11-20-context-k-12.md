# Context of 12 hits instead of 8

With two runs per arm, does assembling the answer's context from the top 12 retrieved hits
instead of the top 8 raise key-fact coverage (EVAL-009) on eval set v2 without lowering EVAL-006?
The question is #36; the approach is the approved comment on #36, choices 1A, 2A and 3A.

#24 found 30 of the 43 standalone misses ranked 9 to 50 (eval set v1). In the seeded baseline
runs EVAL-006 is 0.17 to 0.18 when only some expected articles are in the top 8 and 0.14 when none
are, against 0.47 to 0.48 when all are. More hits reach ranks 9 to 12 with no new model and no API
call.

**The change.** A `--context-k N` option on `python -m src.eval.run` (choice 1A), specified in
`docs/reference/eval-results.md` with failing tests (`8f10ca5`) and built (`6302fb3`).
`AnswerPipeline` retrieves N hits for each standalone question and the item's `hits` hold them
all, while EVAL-001 and EVAL-007 still take the first `TOP_K` (8). Follow-up turns are not
generated and still retrieve 8, so EVAL-005 is unchanged. The run records
`configuration.pipeline.context_k`. The product default and FR-CORE-008 stay at 8.

**Runs.** `python -m src.eval.run --generate --rewrite none --context-k 12 --seed 1`, then
`--seed 2`, at `6302fb3`. Everything as at `e8c4f1b` apart from the context's k: eval set v2
(`f14d299e`), retrieval as at `b0cced1` (bge-m3 `5617a9f6`, `syllable` BM25, `TOP_K` 8, `RRF_K`
60, 50 candidates, one hit per article), `rewrite: none`, answer and judge
`gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, the answer prompt (`2873d56f`) and the judge prompt (`2643e130`) by hash,
budget 24,000, google-genai 3.3.0.

**Baseline:** the seed 1 and seed 2 runs of `e8c4f1b`, as re-scored with EVAL-009 by #39,
`eval/results/2026-11-20-dc5ddea-2.json` and `-3.json`. Both arms draw with the same seeds. Each
run answers every item once, and the judge grades each answer once.

**Decision rule** (in #36, fixed before the runs). Keep 12 when the paired 95% interval of the
EVAL-009 change lies entirely above 0, none of EVAL-006, EVAL-002 and EVAL-003 changes with an
interval entirely below 0, and EVAL-004 stays at or above 0.80. When EVAL-009's interval includes
0, record the result as inconclusive and keep 8; k = 12 is not run again (choice 3A). The flag
stays as an eval option either way (choice 2A).

## Result

Run on 2026-11-21 at commit `6302fb3`, clean, on the dev laptop (i7-10610U, 16 GB RAM, CPU only):
`eval/results/2026-11-21-6302fb3.json` (seed 1) and `eval/results/2026-11-21-6302fb3-2.json`
(seed 2). Answers 1.535 and 1.537 USD; the judge's 156 and 157 calls 0.991 and 0.996 USD.

`python -m src.eval.compare --old eval/results/2026-11-20-dc5ddea-2.json,eval/results/2026-11-20-dc5ddea-3.json --new eval/results/2026-11-21-6302fb3.json,eval/results/2026-11-21-6302fb3-2.json`:

- **Inconclusive, so 8 is kept (choice 3A).** EVAL-009's interval includes 0. The guards pass: no
  interval lies entirely below 0, and EVAL-004 is 0.973. `--context-k` stays as an eval option
  (choice 2A); no default changes, so FR-CORE-008 and *Standing scores* are unchanged.
- **Both runs are valid.** EVAL-001, EVAL-005 and EVAL-007 equal the baseline's on every item, and
  the first 8 hits of every standalone question are the baseline's, as the approach requires.

## Beside the decision

| Not found, answerable, of 184 | 29, 29 | 28, 27 |
| Not found, unanswerable, of 56 | 55, 55 | 54, 55 |
| Answerable items with articles dropped for the budget | 16 | 54 |
| Articles dropped for the budget, in total | 22 | 177 |
| Context tokens per item, median | 12,851 | 19,163 |
| Input tokens per item, median | 14,172 | 21,188 |
| Sentences per answered item, mean (median) | 2.54 (2), 2.53 (2) | 2.38 (2), 2.44 (2) |
| Output tokens per item, mean (median), 240 items | 160 (91), 160 (90) | 152 (98.5), 157 (90) |
| Latency, full pipeline, median and p95 | 2.18 s, 4.57 s; 2.34 s, 6.63 s | 7.24 s, 13.27 s; 3.03 s, 9.00 s |
| Answer cost per item | 0.0050 USD, 0.0050 USD | 0.0064 USD, 0.0064 USD |

- **The per-item split** (184 answerable standalone questions, each item averaged over its arm's
  two runs):
  - **Newly not found:** not-found answers over the two runs fell from 58 to 55. 5 items are not
    found more often at 12 and 7 less often; 5 are not found in both new runs and in neither
    baseline run, 6 the other way round.
  - **More or less complete:** of the 150 items answered in all four runs, 15 state more key facts
    at 12, 13 fewer and 122 the same. These add +0.0061; the other 34 items, not found in at least one
    run, −0.0043.
- **The 23 items that gain an expected article at ranks 9 to 12** carry the gain: their EVAL-009
  rises from 0.357 to 0.426 and their EVAL-006 from 0.152 to 0.217, and their not-found answers
  fall from 12 to 11. They add +0.0086 to EVAL-009; the other 161 items, whose context only gains
  articles that are not expected, take away −0.0068. More passages help where they bring the
  answer and cost a little everywhere else, so the mean barely moves.
- **The budget cuts the gain.** At 12 hits the 24,000-token budget drops articles for 54 items
  instead of 16. For 4 of the 23 items the gained expected article is among those dropped, so only
  19 reach the model. The budget drops from the lowest rank up, so ranks 9 to 12 never displace
  the top 8: the 16 items that lose a top-8 article are the baseline's 16. A larger budget is out
  of scope here.
- **Latency meets NFR-006** (median ≤ 10 s, p95 ≤ 20 s) in both runs. Seed 1's figures include
  retry waits: its last segment met many server errors (503).
- **Cost meets NFR-007** (≤ 0.01 USD per item): the context grows by about half, and the cost per
  item by about a quarter.
- **AC-015:** seed 1 18 pass, 11 fail, 12 retrieval misses, of 41, 7 uncited; seed 2 21 pass,
  9 fail, 12 retrieval misses, of 42, 6 uncited (baseline: 21 pass, 7 fail and 22 pass, 5 fail,
  with 15 retrieval misses each). Not met. Retrieval misses fall by 3, but more of the answers
  fail; the fails of both runs share q01, q37, q56, q117, q197, q199, q200, q204 and q221.
  Citation ids not in the context (AC-009): 1 in 1 item, and 2 in 2 items.

**Hand-check:** none. The owner did not grade the seed 1 sheet (approved on #36). The decision
does not rest on it, since EVAL-009's interval includes 0; whether the judge reads answers from a
longer context more generously (the approach's risk 4) is not measured. #38's hand-check is the
latest.

**How the runs went.** The free tier stopped both runs before they finished; each resumed from
its progress file with the same commit, configuration and seed, and the API key used is not part
of the configuration.

- Seed 1 ran in three segments. The first stopped on the daily request quota of keys 1 to 4 after
  125 items; the second, with two keys of new projects only, stopped on repeated 503s at 146; the
  third resumed the 146 and finished. `run.seconds`, 1,054.1, covers only that last segment.
- Seed 2 stopped on repeated 503s at 146 and resumed with a longer wait between failed attempts
  (`BACKOFF_S` 30 instead of 10, `ATTEMPTS` 8 instead of 5), set in memory by a wrapper, so the
  code on disk and the run's commit are unchanged. `run.seconds`, 1,124.6, covers the last
  segment.
- Seed 1's `run.command` reads `python -m src.eval.run`, without its options, while seed 2's has
  them. Every run launched from the command line records no options (`main` joins `argv`, which is
  `None` then); seed 2 has them because the wrapper passed `argv`. The options are in
  `configuration.pipeline` (`rewrite`, `context_k`, `seed`).

## What follows

8 hits stay. k = 12 is not run again (choice 3A); any rerun is a new issue. The gain lives on the
items whose expected article ranks 9 to 12, and a fifth of those lose it to the budget, so a
larger budget with `--context-k 12` is the next measurable step.
