# ADR-0003: Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite on the free tier generates answers

## Status
Approved by HieuQuang4438 on 2026-11-16.

## Context
FR-CORE-010 needs a model that answers in Vietnamese from the assembled passages, citing a
passage for every claim, or says the documents do not cover the question. NFR-006 asks for a
median ≤ 10 s and a p95 ≤ 20 s from question to answer; NFR-007 for a mean ≤ 0.01 USD per
question at the list price. The project runs without paid services where it can (ADR-0002),
and its demo is public (#18). The top 8 passages hold a median of 7,318 and a p95 of 10,904
bge-m3 tokens (eval set v2, `b0cced1`).

Only Gemini models were compared: one provider and one account, already set up (#13). From
Google AI Studio's pricing and rate-limit pages, read 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16:
- `gemini-2.5-flash`, the scaffold's default, is deprecated; from 2026-12-01 its requests are
  routed to `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`.
- Paid list prices per 1M tokens, output including thinking: `gemini-3.8-flash` and
  `gemini-3.6-flash` 0.75 / 3.75 USD through 2026-12-31, then 1.50 / 7.50 USD;
  `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` 0.30 / 2.50 USD, with no dated change.
- All three have a free tier. Free-tier content may be used to improve Google's products and
  read by human reviewers; paid-tier content is not.
- Rate limits apply per project, not per key, and requests per day reset at midnight Pacific
  time. On the free tier, `gemini-3.6-flash` allows 20 requests per day (a 429 response,
  2026-11-16) and `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` 15 requests per minute and 500 per day (AI Studio
  rate-limit page, 2026-11-16).

A pilot (`scripts/llm_pilot.py`) asked both models the same 24 questions added in eval set v2
(seed 0; per tax type, 3 answerable and 1 unanswerable) over the same top 8, with a minimal
prompt (`scripts/llm_pilot_prompt.md`) and the default generation settings, on the free tier.
The owner graded the answers blind.
- `gemini-3.8-flash`, the current Flash, finished 3 of the 24 items in three runs, at 108 s,
  11.5 s and 128 s, then stopped on 503 UNAVAILABLE and dropped connections
  (`eval/results/llm-pilot/2026-11-16-597534e-partial.json`). `gemini-3.6-flash` replaced it.
- Results, `eval/results/llm-pilot/2026-11-16-e7d3946.json`:

  | | `gemini-3.6-flash` | `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` |
  |---|---|---|
  | Answerable (18): correct / partial / wrong | 13 / 1 / 4 | 11 / 2 / 5 |
  | Score, partial = 0.5 | 0.750 | 0.667 |
  | Answerable answers citing a passage | 14 | 12 |
  | Unanswerable (6) answered "not found" | 6 | 6 |
  | Generation latency, median / p95 | 5.2 s / 21.1 s | 1.4 s / 1.9 s |
  | Output tokens per question, mean, with thinking | 880 | 72 |
  | Cost per question, mean, 2026 / from 2027 | 0.0088 / 0.0176 USD | 0.0024 / 0.0024 USD |

- The two models differ on 2 of the 18 answerable items, which 24 items cannot tell apart.
- Every wrong answer is "not found" on an answerable question. For 4 of Flash-Lite's 5, the
  top 8 held all or all but one of the expected articles, so the minimal prompt makes both
  models refuse too readily.
- Latency is generation alone, from the dev laptop.

Alternatives considered:
- `gemini-3.6-flash`: the better score, but its p95 of 21.1 s breaks NFR-006, its cost from
  2027 breaks NFR-007, and 20 free requests per day serve neither an eval run nor the demo.
- `gemini-3.8-flash`: over 100 s per answer and failed requests on the free tier.
- `gemini-2.5-flash` and `gemini-2.5-flash-lite`: deprecated or the oldest generation listed.
- Other providers (Claude Haiku, an open-weight model on a hosted API): more accounts and
  keys; not measured.
- A paid tier for the demo: fewer quota failures and paid-tier data terms, at a cost the free
  tier caps at zero.

## Decision
Answers are generated by `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` (version `3.5-flash-lite-07-2026` on
2026-11-16) through the Gemini API free tier, for development and the public demo, and the
same model rewrites follow-up questions unless #19 measures otherwise.

## Consequences
- `LLM_MODEL` defaults to `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` in `src/config.py` and `.env.example`. The
  id is an alias Google may move to a newer version, so a results file records the version the
  API reports.
- NFR-007 is computed at 0.30 USD input and 2.50 USD output per 1M tokens (2026-11-16): 0.0024
  USD per question on the pilot, at most 0.0043.
- Free-tier prompts and answers may be used by Google and read by human reviewers. The demo
  says so, asks users not to enter personal data, and shows a clear message when the daily
  quota runs out (#18).
- An eval run with answers sends 292 requests (240 questions and 52 turns), within 500 per
  day if nothing else uses the project that day, and paced under 15 per minute. A run split
  over days says so in its results file.
- Move to a paid tier when the demo runs out of quota on most days, when the free tier's
  limits or terms change so it no longer fits, or when the demo needs data terms that exclude
  product improvement. That reopens the tier, not the model.
- One provider: an outage or a change in Google's terms stops every answer. The fallback is
  another provider's model, a new ADR that touches `src/config.py` and the client.
- Prompt design (#15) starts from the pilot's over-refusal. If #15's prompt reverses the
  ranking on a larger sample, the model is re-checked.
